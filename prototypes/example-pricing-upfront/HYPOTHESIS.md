# Pricing Upfront — Example

## Hypothesis
> Showing the price on the hero increases CTA clicks vs. hiding it behind a Pricing tab.

## Variant
**A** — price visible in the hero subhead.

## Success metric
- Primary: ≥ 15% of `pageview` (path `#home`) result in a `click` with `track == "cta-hero-primary"`.
- Secondary: signup completion rate ≥ 40% of sessions that reach `#signup`.

## Out of scope
- Real backend / persistence
- Authentication
- Variant B (will be added after baseline numbers exist)

Open the [live dashboard](../_shared/dashboard.html?id=example-pricing-upfront) to see metrics. Click around the prototype first to generate sample events.
